# Senior Full-Stack Engineer (PHP/Laravel)

**Location:** Remote (Global/US-based)  
**Team:** Software Engineering

### The Opportunity
We are looking for an experienced Senior Full-Stack Engineer skilled in PHP/Laravel development to help us modernize the way the world handles food safety. Our SaaS platform powers food manufacturers, distributors, and commercial kitchens, ensuring the quality and safety of the food supply chain. Freshability's planned growth is directly linked to the on-time delivery of engineering milestones.

We are at a pivotal moment of scale. We are a small team, and expect your principles and perspectives to shape our product design and delivery processes. Our CI/CD pipeline is nascent, our code coverage is increasing, our Agile practices are evolving, and our tech debt is significant. We are looking for a builder who cares about software modernization, architectural integrity, product maturity, and rapid delivery.

### Role Summary
We are seeking experienced senior engineers who know how to implement software using modern best practices, apply Clean architecture patterns, design engaging UI/UX experiences, practice test-driven development (TDD), and utilize Claude Code as a productivity force-multiplier (but know when its recommendations don't align with existing patterns or architecture choices). You won't just be closing tickets; you'll partner with leadership to evolve our legacy systems into a modern, scalable architecture while shipping critical new features to our growing user base.

### How We Work
We believe in empowering our engineers with the best modern practices. We value:
* **Clean Architecture:** We prioritize long-term maintainability over quick hacks.
* **AI-powered Development:** We expect our engineers to leverage Claude Code for coding, unit testing, documentation, and more, so they can focus their brainpower on high-level system design and simplifying complexity.
* **Accountability:** You own your features from the database schema design to the frontend VueJS component, and everything in between.
* **Initiative:** You independently analyze problems, articulate tradeoffs between solutions, and align on a direction — even if it's not your first choice.

### What You Will Do
* **Modernize and Scale:** Add features and fix bugs within a modern Laravel/Vue architecture. You will solve complex problems regarding data integrity and zero-downtime transitions.
* **Full-Stack Ownership:** Build end-to-end features using PHP (Laravel), TypeScript, VueJS, and TailwindCSS.
* **Elevate Code Quality:** Champion engineering excellence by establishing CI/CD pipelines, writing comprehensive integration tests, and conducting mentorship-focused code reviews.
* **Optimize Performance:** Identify bottlenecks in SQL queries and backend controllers to improve application speed and reliability.

### What You Bring
* **Senior-Level Fluency:** 10+ years of full-stack experience, with a proven track record of implementing modern **PHP** applications while leveraging the **Laravel** ecosystem and community.
* **Software Architecture Expertise:** You independently architect and lead complex features from conception to deployment, mentor other engineers, and make strategic technical decisions that balance speed with long-term maintainability.
* **Frontend Sophistication:** Strong command of modern frontend development (VueJS, Composition API, TypeScript, TailwindCSS). You don't just hack UI; you build reusable component systems.
* **Cloud-native Expertise:** Hands-on experience with containerized deployments (Docker), CI/CD pipelines, and managed cloud services (Digital Ocean, OVH, AWS). You understand how to deploy, monitor, and scale Laravel SaaS applications.
* **Database Mastery:** Advanced proficiency with MySQL, including complex schema design, normalization, and query optimization.
* **AI-first Mindset:** Claude Code is integral to your development workflow — from writing and reviewing code to testing, debugging, and documentation — and you're comfortable leveraging other AI coding assistants when appropriate.

### Preferred Qualifications
* Experience modernizing legacy PHP applications — refactoring toward Clean architecture, introducing domain boundaries, and managing zero-downtime transitions.
* Active open-source contributor.
* Previous experience building, maintaining, deploying, scaling, and supporting a 24x7 production application with web and mobile frontends.
