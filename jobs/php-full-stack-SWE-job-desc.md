# Senior Full-Stack Engineer (PHP/Laravel)

**Location:** Remote (Global/US-based) </br>
**Team:** Product Engineering

### The Opportunity
We are looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to help us modernize the way the world handles food safety. Our SaaS platform powers food manufacturers, distributors, and commercial kitchens, ensuring the quality and safety of the food supply chain.

We are at a pivotal moment of scale. We are looking for a builder who cares about architectural integrity and product maturity. You won't just be closing tickets; you'll partner with leadership to evolve our legacy systems into a modern, scalable architecture while shipping critical new features to our growing user base.

### How We Work
We believe in empowering our engineers with the best modern practices. We value:
* **Clean Architecture:** We prioritize long-term maintainability over quick hacks.
* **AI-Augmented Development:** We encourage the use of AI tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude) to accelerate boilerplate coding, unit testing, and documentation, allowing you to focus your brainpower on high-level system design and complex logic.
* **Autonomy:** You own your features from database schema design to the frontend VueJS component.

### What You Will Do
* **Modernize & Scale:** Add features and fix bugs within a modern Laravel/Vue architecture. You will solve complex problems regarding data integrity and zero-downtime transitions.
* **Full-Stack Ownership:** Build end-to-end features using PHP (Laravel), TypeScript, VueJS, and TailwindCSS.
* **Elevate Code Quality:** Champion engineering excellence by establishing CI/CD pipelines, writing comprehensive integration tests, and conducting mentorship-focused code reviews.
* **Optimize Performance:** Identify bottlenecks in SQL queries and backend controllers to improve application speed and reliability.

### What You Bring
* **Senior-Level Fluency:** 5+ years of full-stack experience, with deep expertise in modern **PHP** and the **Laravel** ecosystem.
* **Frontend Sophistication:** Strong command of modern frontend development (VueJS, Composition API, TypeScript, TailwindCSS). You don't just hack UI; you build reusable component systems.
* **Database Mastery:** Advanced proficiency with MySQL, including complex schema design, normalization, and query optimization.
* **AI Curiosity:** Experience or strong interest in leveraging AI coding assistants to increase velocity and reduce repetitive toil.

### Preferred Qualifications
* Experience with "Strangler Fig" migration patterns or transitioning monolithic apps to microservices.
* Active contributor
